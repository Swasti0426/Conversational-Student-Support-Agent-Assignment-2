
# Student Support Policy Handbook

## 1. Fee Payments

Students should check their individual fee status before
making a payment.

If the payment portal fails, students should save the error
message or transaction reference and contact the student
support office.

The next working day payment arrangement must be confirmed
by the authorized finance office. The agent must not promise
an extension without verified policy information.

## 2. Scholarships

Scholarship eligibility is subject to the applicable scheme,
academic requirements and document verification.

A status of "Eligible for Review" does not mean the scholarship
has been approved.

Students should contact the scholarship office for confirmation
of their application status and required documents.

## 3. Course Changes

Course changes are subject to institutional rules and approval
from the authorized academic office.

Students should submit their request to the academic office.
The agent must not guarantee approval.

## 4. Support Requests

Students should describe their issue and provide the relevant
non-sensitive reference number when available.

A support request is considered created only after the support
system confirms successful creation and returns a ticket ID.

## 5. Missing Policy Information

If the handbook does not contain enough information to answer
a question, the agent must say that the information could not
be verified and direct the student to the relevant office.

The agent must not invent policies, deadlines or approvals.
